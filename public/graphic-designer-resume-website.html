If an Art Director has to squint at light-gray 8pt body copy to find your agency dates, you just failed an information-hierarchy test.

3. Agency clients hire outcomes, not software proficiency.

Pair every rebrand or print campaign with client stage (Series B/C), flagship rollout count, turnaround speedups, or earned media impressions.
