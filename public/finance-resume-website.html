Unpopular Truths Hiring Teams Won't Put in Job Descriptions

1. Never bring a neon sidebar or skill chart into Goldman, Morgan Stanley, or PE recruiting.

Finance recruiting is famously conservative: monochrome serif typography, crisp horizontal rules, and dense transaction bullets are non-negotiable on your PDF.

2. Deal bullets without enterprise value or transaction role look thin.

State the deal size ($1.1B SaaS carve-out), your exact modeling ownership (3-statement, DCF, accretion/dilution), and deal status.

3. Automation in Python/VBA beats manual Ctrl+C / Ctrl+V.

Highlighting how you automated Capital IQ / FactSet comps refreshes immediately separates top associates from spreadsheet grinders.
