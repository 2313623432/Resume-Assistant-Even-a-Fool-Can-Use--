Unpopular Truths Hiring Teams Won't Put in Job Descriptions

1. "Facilitated cross-functional alignment" translates to "I scheduled Zoom meetings."

Every PM talks to engineering and design. Hiring managers want to know what hard trade-off you made, what metric moved, and what happened to retention or revenue.

2. Mentioning a feature you killed makes you sound 10x more senior.

Anyone can write a PRD for another shiny AI button. Showing that cohort data convinced you to sunset three unused widgets and save 25% of sprint capacity screams executive judgment.

3. A 1-page PDF cannot hold your best product thinking.

Keep your ATS PDF razor-sharp at one page, and link your LinkedIn and header directly to a clean personal website where recruiters can inspect your teardowns and 0→1 launches.
