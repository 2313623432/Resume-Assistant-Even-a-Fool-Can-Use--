Unpopular Truths Hiring Teams Won't Put in Job Descriptions

1. If your portfolio site lags or fails keyboard Tab navigation, nobody reads your PDF.

Frontend candidates get silently filtered when their personal site takes 4 seconds to hydrate 3D WebGL blobs. Clean typography, sub-second load speed, and accessible focus states impress senior UI reviewers far more than heavy gimmicks.

2. Stop writing "Pixel-perfect UI" and start writing Core Web Vitals numbers.

Every applicant claims "pixel-perfect responsive design." Stand out by quantifying Largest Contentful Paint (LCP), CLS drops, gzipped bundle savings, and shared component reuse across teams.

3. Do not rate React 90% and CSS 85%.

Arbitrary progress bars make experienced engineers laugh—what does 85% CSS even mean, you forgot how z-index works? Group your stack cleanly by Frontend, Tooling, and Testing.
