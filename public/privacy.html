Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 24, 2026 · Last Updated: November 24, 2026

ResumeCraft is a client-side, privacy-first resume builder and resume-to-website generator. We designed the application so you can create, edit, tailor, and export your resume without creating an account or uploading your personal documents to our servers.

1. Local-Only Resume Storage

All resume fields, uploaded images, theme settings, and imported PDF/DOCX text are processed strictly inside your web browser. Your draft is saved locally in your browser's localStorage so your work persists across page refreshes. We do not operate a backend database and never store or read your resume content.

2. Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) AI Processing

If you use the optional AI Resume Tailoring feature, you supply your own API key (for example, OpenAI, DeepSeek, Google Gemini, OpenRouter, or a custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint).

  • Your API key is saved only in your browser's local storage and can be cleared at any time.
  • Requests are sent directly from your browser to the third-party AI provider URL you configure.
  • Your use of any third-party AI provider is governed by that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

3. Analytics & Tag Management (Google Tag Manager)

We use Google Tag Manager (container GTM-MB3V7PSM) to measure aggregate site traffic and product usage (such as page views and feature clicks) so we can improve ResumeCraft. Google Tag Manager and connected analytics tools may use cookies or pseudonymous identifiers in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy. We do not send your resume text, contact details, or API keys to analytics tools.

4. Your Rights (GDPR / CCPA)

Because we do not collect or store personal resume records on remote servers, you have full, immediate control over your data at all times:

  • Access & Portability: Export your complete resume data as JSON, PDF, or standalone HTML at any time.
  • Deletion: Clear your browser's site data or click "Reset to Sample Resume" to permanently erase all locally stored data and API keys.
  • Cookie Control: You may block or delete cookies via your browser settings or ad/tracker blockers at any time.

5. Exported Resume Websites

When you export your resume as a standalone HTML website and host it on a third-party platform (such as Netlify, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, or GitHub Pages), that published page becomes subject to the hosting provider's privacy practices and your own publishing choices.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to report an issue, please contact us at cjhyqf233@qq.com or open an issue on our GitHub repository.
