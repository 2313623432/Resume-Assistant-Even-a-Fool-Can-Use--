Unpopular Truths Hiring Teams Won't Put in Job Descriptions

1. Titanic, Iris, and MNIST on a 2026 resume are automatic red flags.

Those datasets scream "I completed Week 2 of an intro bootcamp." Replace them with one domain-specific pipeline—like an open-source RAG faithfulness evaluator or a public-data demand forecaster served via FastAPI.

2. Accuracy without business context means nothing.

Writing "Achieved 94% F1 score" leaves recruiters asking "Compared to what baseline, and did it make money?" Pair offline metrics with online A/B lift, latency, or cost impact.

3. Show production deployment, not just model.fit().

Companies hire ML engineers who understand dbt, feature stores, Docker, and monitoring guardrails—not just notebook prototyping.
