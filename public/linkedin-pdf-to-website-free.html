Frequently Asked Questions (LinkedIn PDF to Site · 2026)

Do I need to give my LinkedIn password or install a Chrome extension?

No. Simply use LinkedIn’s built-in "Save to PDF" feature and upload the PDF locally inside your browser.

Is my LinkedIn PDF uploaded to a third-party server?

No. PDF parsing runs 100% client-side in your browser using pdf.js—your personal contact details and work history stay on your device.

Can I also export a cleaner ATS resume PDF from my LinkedIn import?

Yes! LinkedIn’s default PDF layout wastes margins and breaks across awkward pages. After importing, export a crisp Harvard ATS or Modern Minimal PDF for free.

Where can I host the exported website for free?

Netlify Drop, Cloudflare Pages, Vercel, and GitHub Pages all host static HTML portfolio sites for $0/month with automatic HTTPS.
