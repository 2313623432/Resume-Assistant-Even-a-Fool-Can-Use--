Unpopular Truths Hiring Teams Won't Put in Job Descriptions

1. Nobody cares that you know HTML, Git, and VS Code.

Listing "Git, VS Code, Agile, Problem Solving" on a 2026 backend or full-stack resume is the engineering equivalent of putting "Microsoft Word" on an accounting CV. Save space for databases, message queues, and systems you actually operated under load.

2. Your 0-star Todo App does not belong above your internship or prod metrics.

If your Featured Projects section opens with a tutorial clone, recruiters stop scrolling. Replace toy clones with one messy real-world tool: a CLI that saved your team 4 hours a week or an API cache that cut p99 latency in half.

3. Sending only a PDF makes you invisible on LinkedIn and GitHub.

Recruiters and founders check GitHub bios and LinkedIn profiles before opening attachments. Converting your resume into a clean terminal-themed HTML webpage turns cold profile views into warm interview invites.
